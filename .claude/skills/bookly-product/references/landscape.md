# Competitive landscape

Snapshot from general market knowledge, not fresh research. Verify prices and features on the

## Scheduling (Client work)

| Product      | Position                                                                                                                                            | Where Bookly differs                                                                                                                                |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Calendly** | Category leader, booking links, routing, team scheduling, large integration catalogue                                                               | Bookly continues after the booking: contacts, briefings, capture, recaps, tasks, follow-ups, payments; self-hostable                                |
| **Cal.com**  | Open-source (AGPL core with commercial `ee`) scheduling infrastructure, developer focus, platform/API offering                                      | Same licensing model now; Bookly is opinionated about the meeting's before/during/after rather than infrastructure; Bookly video + capture built in |
| **SavvyCal** | Polished booking UX for individuals, overlay of guest calendar, ranked availability                                                                 | Bookly's priority-aware availability (focus blocks, meeting budgets) plus the relationship layer                                                    |
| Adjacent     | Zoom/Teams/Google scheduling, HubSpot meetings, notetakers (Fathom, Otter, Fireflies), lightweight CRMs (Dubsado, HoneyBook for service businesses) | Bookly bundles booking + notetaker + light CRM + payments for people who sell their time                                                            |

## Hiring

| Product                | Position                                                                      | Where Bookly differs                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Greenhouse**         | Enterprise ATS, structured hiring, scorecards, large ecosystem                | Bookly is lighter and cheaper, works for non-job selection (cohorts, grants, programs), scheduling engine native, self-hostable             |
| **Lever**              | ATS + CRM for sourcing and nurture                                            | Bookly treats candidates as people with conversations; no sourcing focus in v2                                                              |
| **Ashby**              | All-in-one ATS + scheduling + analytics for startups, strong reporting        | Bookly's edge is breadth of "hiring" (cohorts, admissions, volunteers) and open core; Ashby is the bar for scheduling quality inside an ATS |
| **Workable**           | SMB ATS with job board distribution and AI sourcing                           | Bookly avoids AI scoring by design; positions on fairness and privacy                                                                       |
| **Teamtailor**         | Employer branding + careers sites + ATS, strong in Europe                     | Bookly's application pages and branding (02 identity, custom domains) compete on careers pages for small teams                              |
| **Submittable**        | Applications, grants, awards, review workflows for nonprofits and foundations | Bookly adds interviews and conversations natively and serves jobs too                                                                       |
| **SurveyMonkey Apply** | Application and review management for scholarships, fellowships, admissions   | Same as Submittable; Bookly's scheduling + capture + messages around the review                                                             |

## Positioning implications

- Nobody owns "the conversations around selecting people" across jobs and programs; ATSs are
  job-centric, application managers are form-centric. That is the gap.
- Open core is table stakes credibility against Cal.com, and a differentiator in hiring where
  almost every ATS is closed.
- Fairness by design (independent reviews, no AI scoring, candidate transparency) is a
  positioning asset in a market under regulatory pressure (EU AI Act, NYC LL 144).
