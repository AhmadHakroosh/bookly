Bookly press kit
================

svg/  Vector files. Use these whenever you can.
png/  High-resolution PNGs (transparent unless the file has its own background).

  bookly-logo           Horizontal logo, orange mark + ink wordmark. The default.
  bookly-logo-on-ink    Horizontal logo on a dark panel.
  bookly-logo-reversed  All-white logo for dark backgrounds and photos.
  bookly-logo-stacked   Mark above the wordmark, for square spaces.
  bookly-mark           The mark alone.
  bookly-mark-reversed  White mark for dark backgrounds.
  bookly-mark-mono      One-colour mark for print.
  bookly-icon           App icon (rounded tile).
  bookly-icon-square    App icon, square, for platforms that round corners themselves.

Colours
  Bookly Orange  #E85D2C   the mark, buttons, links, highlights
  Peach          #FCEAE1   soft fills
  Ink            #1C1C1C   text and dark surfaces
  Paper          #FAF8F4   backgrounds

Please
  - keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of the b's stem;
  - don't recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the logo;
  - don't separate the two bubbles of the mark or retype the wordmark;
  - write the name as "Bookly" in text.

Brand guidelines, product images and contact: https://bookly-app.io/press
